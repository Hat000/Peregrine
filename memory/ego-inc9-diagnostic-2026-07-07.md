## Two methodology footguns burned into this session (do NOT repeat)
- 🚩 **THE RENDER TOOL IS UNTRUSTWORTHY** (`scratchpad/ego_render_rollout.py`). It disagrees with training metrics for BOTH runs in OPPOSITE directions (p2c render=forward-miss vs training=100%coll; p2cfix render=100% floor-dive vs training=28%coll/68%miss).
